# World & Space Clock — Quick-Start Setup Guide

*(Printable 1-page setup card for gift boxes & school fayres)*

## Welcome to Your World & Space Clock!

Your clock tracks the live sun & night across Earth, follows the International Space Station, shows local weather & 3-day forecasts, helps kids know when it's time to wind down, sleep, or play quietly, and celebrates birthdays & holidays automatically!

---

## 3-Step Setup (Takes 2 Minutes on Any Phone)

### Step 1 — Plug In
Connect the supplied **USB-C power cable** into any standard 5V USB power adapter.
- After **15 seconds**, the screen will display a **QR Code** and a Wi-Fi setup name like **`world-clock-a1b2c3`**.

### Step 2 — Connect to Your Home Wi-Fi
1. Open your phone's **Camera** and point it at the **QR Code** on the clock screen (or manually join the Wi-Fi network shown on screen using password **`ClockSetup123`**).
2. A setup page will pop up automatically — pick your home Wi-Fi network and enter your Wi-Fi password.
3. The clock will connect and sync the exact time automatically.

### Step 3 — Personalize Your Clock
Once connected, the clock displays a **second QR Code**, its personal web address, and its unique login right on the screen:
- **Address**: `http://world-clock-XXXXXX.local/` *(unique to your clock!)*
- **Username**: `parent`
- **Password**: `clock-XXXXXX` *(matches the 6 characters on your screen)*

Scan the QR code (or type the address into Safari/Chrome) to customize:
- **Child's Name, Birth Date & Home Town** (plus Timezone & `°C / °F`)
- **4-Phase Sleep Schedule**: Evening Wind-Down (Amber), Deep Sleep (Red with 5 fading stars & real moon phase), Morning Quiet Play (Amber), Weekend & School-Holiday Lie-In, and brightness levels
- **One-Tap Magic**: **Tooth Fairy Tonight!**, **Message of the Day** (auto-clears at midnight), **Nap Timer**, and **Custom Event Countdown**
- **Automatic Family & School Calendars** (optional — see below!)

> **Forgot your clock's address or password?** Just unplug the USB-C cable and plug it back in — your unique URL, IP address, QR code, and login are shown right on the screen every time it starts up!

## Optional: Connect Your Family & School Calendars (Set Once!)

Never touch the clock settings again — let the clock show today's plans (e.g. *"SCHOOL DAY · PE KIT"*, *"SWIMMING (4 PM)"*, or *"NO SCHOOL · HALF TERM"* with automatic lie-in!):

- **Apple iPhone / iCloud Calendar**: Open Apple Calendar → tap **Calendars** → tap `(i)` next to your family calendar → switch on **Public Calendar** → tap **Share Link** → Copy → paste straight into **Family Calendar URL** on the clock!
- **Google Calendar**: Open Google Calendar on the web → Settings → pick your calendar → copy **Secret address in iCal format** → paste into **Family Calendar URL**!
- **School Term Calendar**: Many schools publish a public `.ics` / `webcal://` term-dates link on their website — paste it into **School Term & Holiday Calendar URL** so the clock automatically knows School Days vs. Holidays & Inset Days!
